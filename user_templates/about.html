Starting from a crystal structure (.cif) or a molecular structure (.xyz), the interface identifies molecular components and their connectivity, and determines their chemical properties, including molecular and ligand charges, metal oxidation states, and spin information. Three processing modes (Reference, Unit Cell, and Molecule) allow the analysis of crystallographic data or isolated molecular systems, including structures containing multiple molecular components. The results can be explored through interactive 3D visualizations and downloaded as molecular structures and chemical data for further computational studies.
